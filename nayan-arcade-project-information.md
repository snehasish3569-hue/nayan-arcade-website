## 5. Construction and Building Specifications

### Superstructure

- RCC framed structure designed with a 10-inch finished external wall.

### Wall Finishes

- **Internal:** Birla White putty or equivalent make.
- **External:** Combination of tiles and weather-coat acrylic emulsion paint.

### Flooring

- **Living, dining and bedrooms:** Premium vitrified floor tiles.
- **Master bedroom:** Premium laminated wooden flooring (choice).
- **Staircase:** Granite / step tiles.
- **Driveways:** Heavy-duty tiles.

### Kitchen

- **Flooring:** Premium-quality anti-skid tiles.
- **Wall finishes:** Ceramic tiles up to 2 feet above the working platform; remaining wall in white putty.
- **Counter and sink:** Granite counter with stainless-steel sink.
- **Fittings:** Good-quality chrome-plated sanitary fittings designed to suit a modular kitchen.

### Toilets

- **Walls:** Premium ceramic tiles up to 7 feet.
- **Flooring:** Premium-quality anti-skid tiles.
- **Fittings:** Sanitary fixtures of Jaquar, Hindware or equivalent make; chrome-plated fittings of Jaquar or equivalent make.

### Balcony, Windows and Doors

- **Balcony:** Premium-quality floor tiles and safety steel railing.
- **Windows:** uPVC glass windows.
- **Doors:** Sal wood frame with flush door. The brochure states that the main door will be of teak wood.
